📅 Home Monitoring Protocol (7–14 Days)
Measure twice daily (morning & evening) for 7–14 consecutive days as instructed by your GP. Day 1 readings are discarded. The remaining days are averaged for your result. A home average ≥135/85 mmHg indicates hypertension.
